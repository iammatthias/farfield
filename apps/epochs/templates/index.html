The System

The base unit is one Ethereum block; each concentric epoch is of length 11n blocks, from 111 to 1111 blocks.

Exact earth-times fluctuate based on block-time. For convenience in conceptualizing, ballpark earth-time approximations of epoch lengths are provided, assuming a block time of ~{{.BlockTime}} seconds.
